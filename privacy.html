Privacy policy

StudioOS Personal runs only on its owner's computers and accesses only the owner's own Google accounts, with the owner's consent.

Data read from Google (mail, files, calendar) stays on the owner's computers. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The tool has no other users and collects no data from anyone else.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
